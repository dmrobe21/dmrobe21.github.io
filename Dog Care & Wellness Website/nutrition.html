Dog Nutrition

Good nutrition is an important part of keeping your dog healthy. A balanced diet gives dogs the nutrients and energy they need for growth, activity, and everyday health. The right food and amount can vary depending on a dog's age, size, activity level, and individual needs.

Feeding Guidelines

Dogs should be fed an appropriate amount of food based on their life stage and individual needs. Feeding directions on dog food packaging can provide a starting point, but owners should also pay attention to their dog's weight and body condition.

Puppies usually need more frequent meals because they are growing, while most adult dogs can follow a regular daily feeding schedule. Senior dogs may have different nutritional needs as they become less active or experience age-related changes.

Healthy Treats

Treats can be useful for training and rewarding good behavior, but they should only make up a small part of a dog's overall diet. Giving too many treats can add unnecessary calories and may contribute to weight gain.

Food & Life Stage Guide

Life Stage General Feeding Considerations Helpful Tips
Puppy Puppies need food designed to support growth and development. Feed regular meals and choose food appropriate for the puppy's expected adult size.
Adult Adult dogs need a balanced diet that supports a healthy weight and activity level. Measure portions and adjust feeding based on activity and body condition.
Senior Older dogs may have changing calorie and nutritional needs. Monitor weight and discuss major diet changes with a veterinarian.

Every dog is different. A veterinarian can help determine the most appropriate diet when a dog has special health or nutritional needs.
