Exercise and Physical Activity

Exercise is an important part of keeping dogs healthy and happy. Regular activity helps dogs maintain a healthy weight, use their energy, and enjoy time with their owners. Every dog has different exercise needs depending on age, breed, and health.

Daily Exercise Tips

Walking is one of the easiest ways to keep your dog active. Some dogs enjoy longer walks or hikes, while others prefer shorter outings. Pay attention to your dog's energy level and avoid exercising during extreme heat.

  • Take your dog on regular walks.
  • Always provide fresh drinking water.
  • Allow time for rest between activities.
  • Adjust exercise for puppies and senior dogs.

Activities and Games

Exercise does not have to mean walking the same route every day. Playing fetch, practicing basic commands, and exploring new places are fun ways to keep dogs moving. Choose activities that match your dog's abilities and interests.

Mental Stimulation

Mental exercise is just as important as physical activity. Puzzle toys, scent games, and short training sessions give dogs something to focus on. Even hiding a few treats around the house can turn an ordinary afternoon into a fun activity.
