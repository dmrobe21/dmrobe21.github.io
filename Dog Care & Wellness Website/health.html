Health and Wellness

Keeping a dog healthy involves regular veterinary visits, preventive care, and paying attention to changes in behavior. Learning about your dog's health needs can help you recognize when something may be wrong.

Routine Veterinary Care

Regular checkups allow veterinarians to monitor your dog's health and identify potential problems. During these visits, your veterinarian may check your dog's weight, teeth, ears, heart, and overall condition.

Vaccinations and Preventive Care

Vaccinations help protect dogs from certain infectious diseases. Your veterinarian can recommend a vaccination schedule based on your dog's age, lifestyle, and health history. Flea, tick, and heartworm prevention may also be important.

Recognizing Signs of Illness

Dogs cannot tell us when they feel sick, so owners should watch for unusual behavior or physical symptoms.

  • Changes in appetite or drinking habits
  • Repeated vomiting or diarrhea
  • Unusual tiredness or weakness
  • Coughing or changes in breathing
  • Changes in walking or movement

Contact your veterinarian when you notice concerning symptoms. Difficulty breathing, collapse, or a swollen abdomen with unproductive retching requires emergency veterinary care.

Everyday Wellness Tips

Providing balanced meals, exercise, fresh water, grooming, and a comfortable place to rest can make a big difference in your dog's quality of life. Spending time with your dog also helps build a strong bond.
