Keeping Your Dog's Teeth Healthy

Dental care is sometimes overlooked, but it is an important part of a dog's overall health. Taking care of your dog's teeth can help prevent plaque buildup, bad breath, and painful dental problems.

Brushing Techniques

Brushing your dog's teeth regularly is one of the best ways to maintain good oral hygiene. Use a toothbrush designed for dogs and toothpaste that is safe for pets. Never use human toothpaste.

  • Introduce the toothbrush slowly.
  • Use gentle brushing motions.
  • Focus on the outer surfaces of the teeth.
  • Reward your dog for cooperating.

Dental Treats and Chews

Some dental treats and chews can help reduce plaque and tartar. Look for products that have been evaluated for dental benefits, and always choose an appropriate size for your dog. Supervise your dog while chewing.

Preventing Dental Problems

Regular veterinary dental examinations can help identify problems before they become serious. Watch for persistent bad breath, swollen gums, difficulty eating, or loose teeth. These symptoms should be discussed with your veterinarian.
